## Overview

*Developer Relations Activity Patterns: A Unified Approach to DevRel, DX, and Community Management* documents the 37 activity patterns that recur across Developer Relations teams, with the forces behind each one, when it applies, and how it fails. Published by Apress in 2026.

The pattern catalog is also the foundation for [Agentic Developer Relations](https://agenticairelations.com/), where each of the 37 patterns is reframed for a developer community whose newest members are AI coding agents.
