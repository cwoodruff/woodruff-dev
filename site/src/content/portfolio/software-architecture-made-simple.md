## Overview

*Software Architecture Made Simple: A 'Simplicity-First' Approach to Software in the Age of Complexity* makes the case that complexity is the default failure mode of modern software, and that simplicity has to be chosen on purpose. Published by Apress in 2026, in eBook and softcover.

The book is written for developers, architects, and tech leaders who want systems people can understand, change, and trust. Its companion site is [simplicity-first.dev](https://simplicity-first.dev/), and the ideas keep developing on [The Simplicity-First Philosophy](https://simplicityfirstphilosophy.substack.com/) Substack.
