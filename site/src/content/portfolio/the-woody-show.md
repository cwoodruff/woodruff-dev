## Overview

The Woody Show is a podcast dedicated to exploring the world of software development through candid conversations and practical insights. From architecture patterns to team dynamics, each episode delivers real value for developers and leaders alike.

## What to Expect

- Engaging discussions on software craftsmanship
- Practical tips for developers and architects
- Guest interviews with industry thought leaders
- Explorations of new tools, frameworks, and methodologies
