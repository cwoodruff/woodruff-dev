## Overview

The Breakpoint Show is a podcast where we pause the daily grind to explore the stories, challenges, and breakthroughs in software development. Each episode features conversations with industry leaders, developers, and architects who share their experiences and insights.

## What to Expect

- In-depth conversations with software professionals
- Real-world lessons from the trenches of software development
- Discussions on architecture, best practices, and emerging technologies
- Honest takes on the challenges teams face every day
