## Service Overview

Most growing engineering teams reach a point where the decisions in front of them are too consequential for the loudest voice in the room, but too specific for a generic consultant. A Fractional Architect fills that gap. I embed with your team for a set block of hours each week, sit in on the real conversations, own the architecture decisions that need an owner, and leave your engineers stronger than I found them.

This isn’t staff augmentation. It’s senior technical leadership on a schedule that matches your stage, priced against outcomes—not a headcount line.

## Signals You Need This

- You’ve grown past the point where one founding engineer can hold the whole system in their head
- Architecture decisions are being deferred, reversed, or re-litigated every sprint
- A cloud migration, rewrite, or platform unification is on the near horizon
- You’ve just raised, just been acquired, or just lost a key technical leader
- A full-time principal or staff architect is the right role—but not the right spend yet

## What You Get

- 4–16 hours per week of embedded architecture work, adjustable as needs shift
- Written architecture decision records (ADRs) the team can point to and push back on
- Design reviews, code reviews, and standing hours your engineers can book
- A documented reference architecture and a 90-day technical roadmap
- Hiring input: interview rubrics, candidate debriefs, leveling guidance
- Direct line to leadership with a plain-English translation layer

## How It Works

### Step 1 – Discovery & Alignment

A focused kickoff to map your goals, the constraints you’re operating under, and the systems you already have. You leave the first week with a clear picture of what I’ll be doing and what “better” looks like in 90 days.

### Step 2 – Embedded Engagement

I join your standups, your design reviews, and the Slack channels that matter. I review PRs, write ADRs, and pair on the hardest calls. Typical cadence is 4–16 hours per week, scaled up during transitions and down when things stabilize.

### Step 3 – Iteration & Handoff

As your team matures, my involvement changes shape—deeper during migrations or hires, lighter during steady-state. When it’s time for me to step back, your team has the artifacts and the confidence to run without me.

## Troubleshooting Mode

When systems slow, teams stall, or architectural choices aren’t aging well, I come in with a tighter scope:

- Deep-dive technical assessments with written findings
- Root-cause analysis of architecture bottlenecks and failure modes
- Restructuring guidance with clear sequencing and trade-offs
- Direct, unvarnished recommendations—no filler, no hedging

## Why Work With Me

**Decisions With Receipts** – Every major call is written down with trade-offs, context, and a date. You shouldn’t have to guess why choices were made.

**Mentorship Built In** – I don’t just hand down architecture. I teach your engineers why it works, so the knowledge stays when I leave.

**Business-to-Dev Translator** – I make technical reality legible to leadership and strategic direction legible to engineers.

**Flexible by Design** – I adapt to your pace, team size, and current state. Chaos or calm—same rigor, different dial.
